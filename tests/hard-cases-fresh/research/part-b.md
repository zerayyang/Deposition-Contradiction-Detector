Actual quoted questions are preserved. Each pair has a specific judicial ruling in provenance; none of the judicial rulings are inserted into detector transcripts.

The source snapshots retain only relevant opinion pages, with page markers. Original downloaded PDF SHA-256 and retained-page snapshot SHA-256 are recorded.
