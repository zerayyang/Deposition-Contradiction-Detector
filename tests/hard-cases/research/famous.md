## Limitations and exclusions

- Approximately ten requested; nine supplied. A Pettitte affidavit/Clemens hearing pair was removed because it compared different witnesses. No replacement was invented.
- The Clemens indictment was identified through the [official FBI/DOJ release](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/washingtondc/press-releases/2010/wfo081910.htm), which explicitly states an indictment is not evidence of guilt. A full individually sourced fifteen-statement indictment set was not recovered and is not represented here. These three Clemens rows are not certified as the specific fifteen charged statements.
- No verdict or acquittal proves an individual pair true or false. Bonds reversal concerned insufficient materiality of Statement C; it does not establish the truth of all steroid denials. No pair-specific judicial contradiction finding was imported.
- Public availability is not a blanket open license for all third-party words in a government compilation. The license field identifies public federal records and does not assert ownership or an item-specific open license for witness words. Quotes remain attributed research excerpts.
- The source referral includes its own editorial asterisks/brackets. They are preserved; these source excerpts are not falsely represented as unabridged original transcripts.
- Separate source citations may point to the same compilation because that compilation reproduces both proceedings. The cited proceedings and pages, rather than URL count, distinguish them.
- These files are research drafts, not Level 2 imported bundles or schema-approved author proposals. No automated label approval or gold append occurred.
