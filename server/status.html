What does localhost:3000 mean?

localhost is your own computer. 3000 is the port assigned to this backend. Your visual workspace usually runs on port 5173.

You → app on 5173 → backend on 3000 → Claude

During analysis, the backend calls Claude, validates its extracted quotes, runs deterministic scoring, and adds local Chrono diagnostics.

Available routes

POST /api/analyze accepts two transcript strings from the app:

{"transcript1":"First transcript…","transcript2":"Second transcript…"}
